# Remote Work Policy

## Who can work remotely
Employees on permanent contracts may work remotely up to 2 days per week, agreed with their manager.
Employees on fixed-term contracts may work remotely 1 day per week after their probation period.
Interns may work remotely 1 day per week after their first month, with their tutor's approval.

## How to declare remote days
Remote days are declared in the HR portal before Monday 10:00 for the coming week.
A remote day cannot be moved to another week and is not carried over.

## Equipment and costs
The company provides a laptop and a headset. A monthly allowance of 20 EUR covers internet costs for employees
who work remotely at least 4 days per month. Claims are made with the expenses form.

## Security
Company data is only accessed through the VPN. Working from a public place requires a privacy screen filter,
available from IT on request.
