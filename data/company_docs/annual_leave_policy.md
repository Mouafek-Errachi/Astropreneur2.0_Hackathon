# Annual Leave Policy

## Entitlement
Every employee earns 2.5 days of paid leave per month worked, which is 30 days per full year.
Employees with more than 5 years in the company receive 2 extra days per year.

## Requesting leave
Leave is requested in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for 3 days or more, and 48 hours in advance for
shorter leave. The manager answers within 3 working days.

## Carry-over
Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be taken before 31 March.
Days above this limit are lost unless the manager asked the employee in writing to postpone their leave.

## Sick leave
Sick leave is not taken from annual leave. A medical certificate must be sent to HR within 48 hours.
