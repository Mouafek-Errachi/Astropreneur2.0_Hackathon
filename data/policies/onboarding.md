# Onboarding Policy

## First day
New employees receive their laptop, badge and accounts on the first day. Their manager assigns an onboarding buddy.

## Probation period
The probation period is 3 months for permanent contracts and 1 month for fixed-term contracts.

## Onboarding checklist
HR sends the checklist one week before the start date: signed contract, ID copy, bank details form and emergency contact.
