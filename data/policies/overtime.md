# Working Hours and Overtime Policy

## Working hours
The standard week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday, with core hours from 10:00 to 16:00.

## Overtime
Overtime must be approved in advance by the line manager. It is compensated as time off in lieu within 2 months, or paid at 125% if time off is not possible.

## Weekend work
Weekend work requires director approval and is compensated at 150%.
